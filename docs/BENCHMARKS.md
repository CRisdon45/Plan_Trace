# Public-safe benchmark specifications

Status: **specified, not implemented fixtures or accepted results**. Recorded 2026-09-10 from private project references and the owner's workflow explanation. IDs are public aliases; no client names, addresses, source-file names, exact lot plans or photographs belong here. Use original synthetic geometry for public test projects. Permission/redaction rules are in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md).

## SITE-01: start the site here

Purpose: remove the preliminary use of another design application. Begin with synthetic overhead/plan imagery or a measured outline, not a pre-authored completed pool project.

Exercise import, source orientation, known-distance calibration, another available reference check, a house outline with perpendicular runs and exact lengths, patio, walls/access and existing features. Preserve traced/assumed/verified status. Fade or hide the source, begin a design in the same project, save, reopen and export.

Pass when source registration and units remain truthful, the base remains editable/protected as intended, design objects do not depend on keeping another app open, and output agrees with the working view. The private initial-site reference did not supply the original overhead image or independent measurements; do not pretend exact reconstruction has been verified.

## ORTHO-01: composed geometric yard

Include a geometric pool with projecting shelf/steps and connected coping, edge-specific raised wall sections, repeated bowls/sheers, adjoining paving with inset pads/turf gaps, planted gravel, a shade/media-wall footprint, and front/side/back-yard scope objects.

Exercise lengthening the pool; widening the shelf; adjusting a fixed-outer-edge deck region; changing a turf gap/pad rhythm; redistributing an explicitly linked bowl group; moving an unrelated plant; comparing an alternative; and changing materials. Quantities, attachments and intended alignments should follow without repairing disconnected artwork. The unrelated plant must not move when another assembly changes.

The completed-project photos illustrate physical relationships and composition, not measured dimensions or a proven as-built revision. This is a domain/workflow benchmark, not an instruction to reproduce construction-sheet styling.

## ORGANIC-01: flowing full-yard design

Include concave and convex pool arcs/curves with tangent transitions, following coping, interior shelf/steps, adjoining curved deck/lawn, circular seating/fire-pit zone, a waterfall footprint, outdoor kitchen/shade, planting and lighting.

Exercise reshaping one lobe, changing a curve radius/handle with the chosen anchor, adjusting a shared curved surface edge, preserving interior attachments, comparing an alternative, and switching working/presentation views. Check continuity, valid offsets/holes, meaningful dimensions/perimeters, unchanged unrelated objects and no cracks/double counting between surfaces.

The private pool and landscape sheets were different revisions, and one landscape sheet was marked under review/not for construction. They are not synchronized numerical ground truth or recordings of the first meeting.

## Common acceptance matrix

For each implemented fixture, record its ID/version and synthetic provenance, supported operations, explicit expected invariants, numeric tolerances, actual source revision, and results for creation, dependency editing, whole-action Undo/Redo, save/reopen and output. Include actual screen/export captures only after publication review. Missing steps remain visibly pending.

Use authentic renderer output at full sheet and detail zoom. Confirm Technical/Graphic/Northstar preserve design geometry and that live editing does not rerandomize materials/plants. Test orientation/handedness and the physical radial/pen flow on the tablet separately from geometry tests. Timings compare the entire task against the owner's practiced workflow, including preparation and cleanup.

## Source truth versus calculation

Keep source ID/revision/status, source-stated values, and independently derived quantities distinct. Private references include both a cross-revision paving-total change and an inconsistent written arithmetic total. Do not average them, silently correct them, or tune new geometry to force an unexplained match. Public regression fixtures should use deliberately chosen synthetic numbers with independently checkable expectations, not private project quantities.
