# Northstar visual contract

Reconciled 2026-09-10. Target art direction, not an implemented-renderer claim. The original detailed specification and starting palette remain available in [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md).

## Goal and reference roles

Create a deterministic ink/marker/wash landscape-plan language driven by editable objects. The design should look intentional from its first meaningful objects and stay attractive during revisions. The pool/water is ordinarily the visual focus in a pool project. Do not force every layout to match the density or circular water feature in the supplied garden references.

The five recorded Northstar images are one visual family: a full composition plus details, not five independent project benchmarks. Their filename locators and availability are in [references/manifest.json](references/manifest.json). Their pixels are not committed here. This context update preserves the earlier reference interpretation; it does not claim a new pixel-level review. Missing originals must be disclosed before visual sign-off.

Construction sheets establish content/geometry, not aesthetic goals. Completed-project photos clarify composition and physical relationships, not dimensions or verified as-built revisions. Screenshot buttons, device chrome and gallery overlays are not intended UI or artwork. Never substitute reference art, a generated mockup or a hard-coded scene image for actual renderer output.

## Three views, one design

**Technical:** clean paper, controlled black/gray geometry, minimal fills and deliberate dimensions.

**Graphic:** material-aware line hierarchy, restrained flat tones, clear water and designed plant silhouettes.

**Northstar:** the same geometry with bounded wash variation, richer planting detail, selective depth and texture.

Mode changes preserve scale, topology, annotation associations, history and export alignment. Styling never changes measured boundaries. Legacy strokes keep their explicit appearance unless the user assigns meaning. The working canvas uses this system, not a separate low-quality renderer followed by a final beautification filter.

## Visual hierarchy

Pale open surfaces need substantial selective darks in built edges and planting. Restrained does not mean everything becomes pale, low-contrast or timid. Concentrate detail and darkness where they explain form. Preserve white gaps and readable circulation.

Use stronger structural/perimeter outlines, lighter secondary edges, quieter paving joints and subdued dimensions/leaders. Stable hand-rendered warmth is optional; universal cross-corners, excessive overshoots and random wobble are not substitutes for design quality.

Starting physical weights may use the earlier proposal: primary boundaries about 0.50 mm, walls 0.45/0.18 mm outer/internal, pool 0.40 mm, coping/deck 0.25 mm, material joints 0.10 mm, dimensions/leaders 0.13 mm. These are tunable proposals, not sampled facts or finalized requirements. Screen/output transforms must preserve their intended printed hierarchy. Interaction-only legibility aids do not silently change exports.

## Materials and plants

Water is clipped to valid geometry, with restrained blue-green tone and broad depth cues. Shelves, steps and benches remain legible. Avoid animated caustics and photoreal noise in the initial 2D treatment.

Turf uses broad irregular tonal variation, retained paper highlights and soft pigment edges. A diagonal gradient alone does not achieve the reference. Do not add high-frequency speckles or visible repeated tile seams.

Paving/coping maintain exact boundaries and coherent pattern orientation/scale. Joints are subordinate to outer geometry and simplify at sheet zoom without changing design dimensions. Gravel stays a quiet field. Planting eligibility does not require a decorative bed boundary.

Use distinct tree, palm, shrub, succulent/accent, flowering and groundcover silhouettes. Bounded lobes/fronds, intentional negative space and selective near-dark masses create form. Avoid branch spaghetti, generic translucent blobs and evenly distributed leaf noise. Repeatable variation should not make every instance identical or every movement redraw the canopy. The symbol must remain recognizable during edits.

Shadows explain height, contact and overlap using one consistent illustrative light direction. They are presentation cues unless separately calibrated as a sun study. Cap accumulated darkness. Grass does not cast tree-like shadows. Raised walls and underwater features need different, restrained depth cues.

## Rendering integrity

Use shared document/world-to-sheet transforms for registered sources, geometry, preview, PNG and PDF. Maintain explicit bounds, margins and source registration. Render ground fields first, then relevant structural lines and ordered raised-object/shadow groups, planting groups, and labels. Final boundary ink must remain legible; selection handles and editing guides never export.

Anchor patterns and variation to stable object/style data and an explicit seed, not frame time, zoom, render order or resolution. Reopening, panning, zooming or editing a neighbor must not rearrange wash patches or leaves. Cache against geometry/style revisions. Shared boundaries must not create doubled outlines or inconsistent pattern joins.

Labels, legends, north direction and graphic scale remain readable at actual output size. Reserve calm margins and avoid collisions. Use temporary panels rather than permanent UI clutter over the drawing. UI colors, palette tokens and exact shadow values remain tunable, not a mandate to reproduce a prototype.

## Acceptance

Review actual Technical, Graphic and Northstar output for both ORTHO-01 and ORGANIC-01 at full sheet, normal working zoom and detail zoom, during editing and after reopen. Inspect actual PNG/PDF outputs as well as the canvas. Check unchanged boundaries and quantities, stable patterns, readable plants, coherent shadows, restrained detail, clear water/circulation and collision-free annotations.

Style acceptance requires authentic captures tied to a source revision and the actual visual references or an explicit owner-approved replacement. Geometry tests can proceed while image access is missing; do not claim the Northstar is matched in that condition. Visual quality develops alongside the first complete editing workflows, while trustworthy geometry and output remain prerequisites rather than defects hidden by styling.
