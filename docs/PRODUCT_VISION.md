# Product direction: design the project with the client

Adopted direction, 2026-09-10. This document describes the destination, not today's implemented capabilities. Current evidence and work authorization live in [CURRENT_STATE.md](CURRENT_STATE.md).

## The promise

**Enable Cody to create, revise, explain, and preserve a complete pool-and-landscape design during a client meeting, more fluently than his own experienced Pool Studio workflow. Keep the design attractive throughout the conversation.**

Cody already designs and sells substantial projects in first meetings. The app is not teaching a beginner to draw. It should remove software work that interrupts an experienced designer's judgment. It is a personal professional instrument, not a commercial SaaS product or an attempt at general CAD feature parity. Plan Trace is a working name, not a tracing-only scope.

Satellite/image tracing, importing a plan, and drawing a measured site from scratch are entry routes. The editable outdoor project is the product. Site preparation must not require a preliminary Pool Studio session.

## What a successful meeting looks like

Open or create the site. Establish the house, patio, property/site boundaries, access, existing features, orientation, and the confidence of measurements. Explore the pool and whole-yard composition with the client. Change a pool edge, shelf, surface boundary, or planting arrangement without repairing unrelated artwork. Compare an alternative without losing the original. Show an attractive uncluttered view, save the agreed design, reopen it reliably, and hand off useful drawings and quantities.

The pool, its coping, the deck, and their dimensions must not be disconnected colored marks. At the same time, loose sketching and temporary discussion marks remain available without formally classifying every stroke.

## Complete-design scope

The target includes pool/spa geometry and interior features; coping and raised edges; decking, turf, gravel and planting; walls and water/fire features; shade and outdoor-living footprints; major equipment/utility locations; materials, relevant clearances, design alternatives, and traceable quantities. Front, side and back yards are part of the same site.

Meeting completeness is not a promise of automated permitting, structural certification, electrical design, or hydraulic engineering. Preserve unresolved facts and scope explicitly. A persuasive plan must not imply that unverified measurements or engineering are approved.

## Product boundaries

- Pen-first tablet design is the priority. Radial commands, exact local controls, safe navigation, and direct manipulation serve that workflow.
- Technical, Graphic and Northstar appearances are views of the same geometry. Northstar quality belongs in the working canvas, not only in a final export filter.
- Rectilinear and concave/convex freeform projects must shape the foundation early. A rectangle-only demonstration is insufficient.
- Runtime behavior is deterministic and local-first. No shipped AI assistant, generative designer, LLM dependency, or AI service requirement. GPT-6 Astra is the owner's development-assistance choice, not an application dependency.
- 3D remains paused. Retain meaningful height/depth data in 2D without restarting the perspective work.
- Decorative granite can contain plants. A separate planting-bed object is not a prerequisite. Support explicit no-plantings and plantings-only areas when needed.
- Keep existing project data safe. Do not replace the framework or merge other product repositories merely to pursue this vision.

## What we borrow

Concepts contributes the inspiration for compact radial access, editable drawing and precision near the work. Morpholio Trace contributes fluid scaled exploration, overlays and architectural communication. Pool Studio contributes useful pool/landscape domain behavior and reusable assemblies. These are design inspirations selected by the owner, not requirements to copy assets, whole interfaces or every feature. Improve an interaction when the meeting workflow benefits.

## The quality test

Measure the full workflow against Cody's practiced process: preparation, initial composition, revisions, visual cleanup, saving, and handoff. Speed, frame rate and latency targets must be established and measured on the target tablet; no improvement percentage is claimed yet. Preserve accuracy and recoverability while reducing effort.

**Decision rule: does this make it easier to create, understand, or change the project while the client is sitting here?**
